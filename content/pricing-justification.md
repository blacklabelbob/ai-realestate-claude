## 1. Cost of Being Wrong

### Buyer Regret & Overpaying

- 82% of 2023–2024 home buyers have at least one regret about their purchase — **82%** ([Clever Real Estate, 2024 American Home Buyer Report](https://listwithclever.com/research/homebuyer-sentiment/))

- The average buyer targeted a purchase price of $483,490 — but actually spent around $542,000 — **12% above target** ([Clever Real Estate, 2024 American Home Buyer Report](https://listwithclever.com/research/homebuyer-sentiment/))

- 37% of buyers believe they overpaid for their home; among first-time buyers it rises to 42% — **37% / 42%** ([Clever Real Estate, 2025 American Home Buyer Report](https://listwithclever.com/research/homebuyers-survey-2025/))

- 65% of all buyers have regrets; 73% of first-time buyers experience post-purchase regret — **65% / 73%** ([Clever Real Estate, 2025 American Home Buyer Report](https://listwithclever.com/research/homebuyers-survey-2025/))

- 38% of buyers exceeded their initial budget when purchasing; 47% of first-time buyers blew their budget — **38% / 47%** ([Clever Real Estate, 2025 American Home Buyer Report](https://listwithclever.com/research/homebuyers-survey-2025/))

- 45% of homeowners have regrets about their current home, with maintenance costs and hidden fees as the #1 complaint — **45%** ([Bankrate, May 2025 Homeowner Regrets Survey](https://www.bankrate.com/real-estate/homebuyer-regret-survey-may-2021/))

- 66% of first-time homeowners faced unexpected issues after buying, with an average cost of $5,356 — **$5,356 average surprise cost** ([The Mortgage Point, April 2025](https://themortgagepoint.com/2025/04/23/first-time-homebuyers-facing-regret-pressure-and-hidden-costs/))

- 63% of recent buyers (2025 Realtor.com survey) experienced some form of post-purchase regret; only 37% had no regrets — **63%** ([Realtor.com / PR Newswire, May 2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/realtorcom-as-market-cools-buyer-regret-does-too-302621210.html))

### Wrong Location / Neighborhood

- 14% of homeowners with regrets specifically cite buying in a bad location — **14%** ([Bankrate, 2025 Homeowner Regrets Survey](https://www.bankrate.com/real-estate/homebuyer-regret-survey-may-2021/))

- Those pressured into a fast decision were nearly 3x more likely to experience buyer's remorse — **3x more likely** ([The Mortgage Point, April 2025](https://themortgagepoint.com/2025/04/23/first-time-homebuyers-facing-regret-pressure-and-hidden-costs/))

### Hidden Ongoing Costs

- The average annual cost of owning and maintaining a typical U.S. single-family home is $21,400 in 2025 (excluding mortgage) — **$21,400/year** ([Bankrate, Hidden Costs of Homeownership Study 2025](https://www.bankrate.com/home-equity/hidden-costs-of-homeownership-study/))

- Home maintenance alone accounts for approximately $8,808 of that annual total — **$8,808/year** ([Bankrate, Hidden Costs of Homeownership Study 2025](https://www.bankrate.com/home-equity/hidden-costs-of-homeownership-study/))

## 2. Cost of a Bad Rental / Investment Decision

### Negative Cash Flow Risk

- Investors who skip proper cash flow analysis risk losing $5,000–$15,000 annually on poorly performing properties — **$5,000–$15,000/year** ([Steadily, "What is the average cash flow on a rental property?"](https://www.steadily.com/blog/what-is-the-average-cash-flow-on-a-rental-property))

- Each month a property sits vacant equals 8–10% of annual rental income lost — and the true cost of vacancy is roughly **2x lost rent** when carrying costs (mortgage, taxes, insurance, utilities, turnover) are included — **2x multiplier** ([Findigs, "How to Calculate the Cost of Vacancy Loss"](https://www.findigs.com/blog/how-to-calculate-the-cost-of-vacancy-loss-and-raise-the-bar-on-occupancy))

- The national residential rental vacancy rate was 6.6% in Q1 2024 — meaning the average landlord is already losing ~7% of potential income to vacancy before any repair surprises — **6.6% vacancy** ([Azibo, "Calculating and Understanding Your Rental's Vacancy Rate 2024"](https://www.azibo.com/blog/rental-vacancy-rates))

### Unexpected Repairs / CapEx

- In a survey of 5,000+ landlords, 54.97% reported their most expensive single repair in the past year exceeded $2,000; an additional 20.17% had repairs between $1,000–$2,000 — **55% faced a $2,000+ repair** ([Bay Management Group, "Rising Costs Are Changing Rental Housing," 5,000+ Landlord Survey](https://www.baymgmtgroup.com/blog/rising-costs-are-changing-rental-housing/))

- 46.13% of landlords identify maintenance costs as their biggest financial pressure; repairs account for ~35.66% of total operating expenses — **46% say it's their #1 pressure** ([Bay Management Group, "Rising Costs Are Changing Rental Housing"](https://www.baymgmtgroup.com/blog/rising-costs-are-changing-rental-housing/))

- 26.27% of landlords maintain no emergency reserve fund at all, leaving them fully exposed to unexpected repair costs — **1 in 4 landlords, zero reserves** ([Bay Management Group, "Rising Costs Are Changing Rental Housing"](https://www.baymgmtgroup.com/blog/rising-costs-are-changing-rental-housing/))

- Standard industry guidance: budget 1%–2% of property value annually for maintenance — on a $350,000 property that is $3,500–$7,000 per year in reserves required — **1–2% of value rule** ([Belong Home, "Rental Property Maintenance Costs in 2025"](https://belonghome.com/blog/rental-property-maintenance-costs))
