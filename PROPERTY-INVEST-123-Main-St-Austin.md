## STRATEGY 2: BRRRR (Buy-Rehab-Rent-Refinance-Repeat)

### Verdict: NOT VIABLE for this property type

**Why downtown 78701 condos fail BRRRR:**

1. **No forced appreciation lever.** BRRRR depends on creating equity via rehab. Downtown condos: (a) HOA controls building exterior, common areas, hallways, mechanicals — investor can't touch them; (b) interior finishes have a tight ceiling because comps in the same building set value; (c) you cannot add square footage or bedrooms.

2. **Refi math doesn't work.** ARV after a $40K interior gut might be $730-750K vs. $700K purchase — gain of $30-50K. After 75% LTV refi, you recover maybe $22-37K of additional capital. You still leave $160-180K trapped in the deal.

3. **HOA lock-in.** Many downtown buildings (Austonian, Independent, 360, Spring) require HOA approval for renovations and have rules about contractor hours/freight elevator scheduling that drag rehab timelines from weeks to months.

4. **Buyer's market at 8.8 months supply** means appraisers are using comps with rapidly declining prices — refi appraisals are coming in light across Austin.

5. **Repeat step is broken.** The whole point of BRRRR is to recycle capital into the next deal. Capital trapped in a downtown condo doesn't recycle.

## STRATEGY 3: FIX & FLIP

### Verdict: NOT VIABLE in current market

**Why this fails:**

1. **108 days average days-on-market for Austin condos** (March 2026). Flippers need 30-60 day exits. Downtown 78701 specifically scores 10/100 on Redfin's competitiveness scale — listings sit.

4. **No forced-appreciation lever** (same constraints as BRRRR).

## TOP 3 RISKS

### 1. Interest Rate Risk (Severity: HIGH)

Investment property rates currently 7.0-7.6% (Bankrate, May 2026). Each 100bps move:
- Up: kills refi optionality, deepens negative cash flow at variable expenses
- Down: 5.5% rates would shift this from negative cash flow to break-even and create instant equity via lower cap-rate compression

**Mitigation:** Lock 30-yr fixed; build refi war chest; consider 7/1 ARM at lower rate if planning 5-yr exit.

### 2. Austin Market Correction Risk (Severity: HIGH)

Austin had the **4th-largest home price decline in the U.S. since 2025** (CultureMap/SmartAsset). Downtown 78701 condos specifically:
- 8.8 months of supply (deep buyer's market)
- 6.3% YoY price decline
- Construction projects being paused/delayed (KXAN)
- Tech sector layoffs continuing to pressure local incomes

**Worst-case scenario:** Another 10-15% downtown condo decline over 18 months puts owner $80-105K underwater on equity.

**Mitigation:** Negotiate purchase 15-20% under list to create margin of safety; plan 7-10 year hold to ride through cycle.

### 3. Regulatory Risk — STR Restrictions (Severity: MEDIUM-HIGH)

Austin STR regulations tightened materially in 2025-2026:
- Residential-zoned condos: only **3% of building units** can operate as STRs
- Effective July 1, 2026: platforms must remove unlicensed listings within 10 days
- $500/day fines for non-compliance
- 11% Hotel Occupancy Tax now collected at platform level

**Translation:** The STR/Airbnb income upside that downtown Austin condo buyers historically counted on is largely gone. If your underwriting depended on $200+/night nightly rates, the deal collapses.

**Mitigation:** Underwrite to long-term rental rates only ($4,100/mo); treat any STR upside as a bonus, not a base case.

### Honorable Mentions

- **HOA special assessments** — aging downtown towers face $20-50K assessments for facade, elevators, mechanicals. Get the reserve study before buying.
- **Property tax appraisal increases** — Travis County values up 3.6% in 2026; investment properties have no homestead cap.
- **Insurance premium escalation** — Texas hard market continues; HO-6 premiums up 15-25% year-over-year statewide.
