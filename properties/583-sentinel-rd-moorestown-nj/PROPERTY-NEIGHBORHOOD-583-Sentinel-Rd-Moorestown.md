## Executive Summary

583 Sentinel Rd sits in **Stanwick Glen**, an established residential pocket of Moorestown Township — a high-income, top-rated suburban community ~25 minutes from Center City Philadelphia. Moorestown consistently ranks among New Jersey's best-graded school districts (Niche A+, #16 statewide) and has below-average violent crime, very high educational attainment (64%+ Bachelor's or higher), and 81%+ owner-occupancy. - Days on market: 28–35 days for well-priced homes (South Jersey average)

**Population:** +2% since 2020 — **slow but positive**. Growing 0.83% annually. Only ~1% of housing stock is post-2020 construction — Moorestown is essentially **built-out**, which structurally constrains supply and supports prices.

**3–5 Year Outlook (2026–2031):**
- **Base case: +3% to +5% per year** appreciation. Constrained supply + premium school district + Philly-metro spillover demand support continued price strength.
- **Headwinds:** Mortgage-rate sensitivity at the $700K–$1M+ price band; NJ has the highest property-tax burden in the U.S. (Moorestown effective rate ~2.4–2.7%); appreciation has historically lagged the national average (~5.1% CAGR over 10 years per NeighborhoodScout).
- **Tailwinds:** Schools moat, finite developable land, top-1% income demographic that is recession-resilient, return-to-office demand for Philly-commute suburbs.

**Verdict:** Solid long-term appreciation play, **not** a high-velocity speculative market. Best for primary residence or long-hold rental; weaker for short-flip strategies.

## 9. Top 3 Strengths

1. **Elite, A+ school district** — #16 in NJ, top-rated at every grade level. Schools alone justify a meaningful price premium and provide rental-demand insulation.
2. **High-income, owner-occupied, educated population** — $158K+ median HHI, 64% bachelor's+, 81% owner-occupied. This is a recession-resilient demographic that supports stable home values and low default risk.
3. **Constrained supply + finite buildable land** — only ~1% post-2020 construction; township is effectively built-out. Combined with desirable brand, this provides structural price floor support.

## 10. Top 3 Weaknesses / Concerns

1. **Car-dependency and weak transit** — no rail station, modest Walk Score, mandatory two-car household. Limits buyer pool to those who don't need rail commute and increases lifestyle cost (auto, fuel, maintenance).
2. **High NJ property taxes** — Moorestown's effective rate (~2.4–2.7%) is among the highest in the U.S. On a $700K home that is ~$17K–$19K/yr, materially impacting affordability and total carry cost.
3. **Flood-zone proximity** — Pennsauken Creek tributaries and Strawbridge Lake are nearby. Specific parcel must be checked; even outside FEMA SFHA, climate-adjusted flood risk is rising per First Street modeling.
