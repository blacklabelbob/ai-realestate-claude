## 5. NJ-Specific Headwinds

### Property tax pressure
- NJ has the **highest effective property tax rate in the U.S. (~2.47%)**.
- Moorestown SFRs at $700K+ commonly carry **$13K–$20K+ annual property tax bills**. This is a permanent affordability ceiling — every 50 bps of rate move materially shifts the qualifying buyer pool.

### SALT cap — TAILWIND through 2029, then cliff
- The One Big Beautiful Bill Act lifted the SALT cap from **$10,000 → $40,400 (2026)**, indexed +1% annually through 2029.
- For NJ buyers earning under $500K MAGI, this **restores meaningful federal deductibility** of property + state income tax — a tangible 5-figure annual benefit on Moorestown-priced homes.
- **Caveat:** Cap reverts to $10K in 2030. Today's buyer gets ~3–4 years of full benefit before a possible re-pricing event. This creates a **near-term demand pull-forward**.

### Out-migration
- NJ ranked #7 nationally for net out-migration per capita in 2025 (-16,283 net residents).
- 192,209 net residents left since 2020; lost $2.55B in AGI in just 2022–23.
- NJ "exit withholding" (8.97% of profit OR 2% of sale price, whichever higher) is a friction at sale — **buyers should model this if they may leave NJ within 5–7 years**.

## 7. Seasonality

- NJ market follows classic **spring peak (Apr–Jun)** with highest list count, fastest DOM, strongest list-to-sale ratios.
- **Summer plateau** Jul–Aug.
- **Fall secondary peak** Sep–Oct (school-year families who missed spring).
- **Winter trough** Dec–Feb — *best leverage window for buyers*; fewer competing offers, motivated sellers carrying through holidays.
- **For 583 Sentinel Rd in May 2026:** You are buying at the **annual peak of competition**. Expect tight negotiation room. If timeline is flexible, a Nov–Jan 2027 entry would offer better terms.
