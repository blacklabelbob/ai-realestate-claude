## 7. Short-Term Rental (STR / Airbnb) Analysis

### Regulatory Reality (CRITICAL)

Austin licenses STRs through Austin Development Services Code Compliance. Three license types exist:

- **Type 1:** Owner-occupied. Not viable for an investor purchase.
- **Type 2:** Entire dwelling, non-owner-occupied. **Historically restricted; new licenses have been heavily limited or capped in residential zones.** As of 2025-2026, Austin tightened enforcement, and starting **July 1, 2026**, Airbnb / VRBO / Booking.com must display a valid Austin STR license number on every listing and remove unlicensed properties within 10 days of city request.
- **Type 3:** Multi-family non-owner-occupied (some downtown condo buildings).

**Many downtown condo HOAs explicitly prohibit STR**, even where the city would allow it. The Austonian, The Independent, and several luxury towers have HOA bans on stays under 30 nights. **Verifying the HOA's CC&R rental policy is mandatory before underwriting STR upside.**

**License processing:** 4-6 weeks (Type 1/2), 8-10 weeks (Type 3).

**Hotel Occupancy Tax (HOT):** 17% total (9% city + 2% city venue + 6% state). Platforms now collect city HOT automatically as of April 1, 2025; operator still files quarterly.

## 9. Top 3 Risks

### Risk 1 — Negative Cash Flow Sustainability (HIGH)
- $42K-$50K annual cash burn requires deep reserves. A single vacancy month + special HOA assessment can compound losses.
- **Mitigation:** Increase down payment to 40-50% to reach break-even; OR wait for rates to drop to 5.5% (would shave ~$650/mo P&I).

### Risk 2 — STR Regulatory & HOA Restriction (HIGH if STR is the plan)
- The July 1, 2026 platform rule will scrub unlicensed listings within 10 days of city notice — meaning any underwriting that depends on "fly under the radar" STR is dead.
- Downtown condo HOAs frequently ban stays under 30 nights regardless of city license status.
- **Mitigation:** Pull the HOA CC&Rs and verify Type 2/3 license eligibility BEFORE closing. If either fails, STR upside is zero.

### Risk 3 — Austin Market Softening / Rent Compression (MEDIUM)
- Austin had +11% YoY 1BR rent growth in downtown but the broader Austin metro saw rent declines in 2024 from over-supply of new Class A apartments. Downtown condos compete directly with new luxury rentals.
- 78701 sale-side is up 17% YoY (Feb 2026) — concentrated downtown bull run that may revert if tech employment in Austin softens.
- **Mitigation:** Stress-test underwriting at -10% rent and 3-month vacancy to validate worst-case reserves.
